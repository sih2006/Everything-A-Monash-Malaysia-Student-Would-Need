# Prototype

A clickable example of how the app could look. It is **not** the real app. It uses made-up data and none of this code will be reused.

## How to open it

Open `index.html` in a browser. There is nothing to install.

## What you can try

- **Home:** a summary of the week and a list of things that need attention.
- **Map:** four paths (studies, budget, food, health) that run from left to right. Drag to move, zoom in and out, and pick a stop to see its details.
- **Grades:** GPA, CGPA and WAM, your final goal, and each unit with two verdicts (possible, realistic). Move the slider to see your final mark for any exam score.
- **Budget:** add spending and watch the totals change.
- **Food:** tap a food to log it.
- **Health:** steps, sleep, heart rate and workouts.
- **Hide grades:** hides every mark, grade and target.
- **Movement:** screens fade and rise in, bars and charts grow, and the map path draws itself. All of it is turned off if your device asks for less motion.
- **Look:** switch between four looks: tropical print, sticker book, night market and classic.
- **Dark mode:** switches between light and dark.

Changes you make are lost when you reload the page.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page and the menu |
| `styles.css` | Colours, type and layout for the default look |
| `looks.css` | The other three looks |
| `data.js` | All the fake data |
| `app.js` | The rules and the screens |

## About the data

Everything in `data.js` is invented.

- The unit codes are examples. The assessment weights and hurdles are made up, not taken from the Monash handbook.
- The grade bands and grade points are not checked against Monash yet.
- The WAM here is a simple average weighted by credit points. The real Monash formula may be different.
- The calorie and protein numbers are rough guesses.

## About the mascots

There are no mascot pictures yet. Each mascot has an empty box with its name and mood. The real art will come from the artist.
